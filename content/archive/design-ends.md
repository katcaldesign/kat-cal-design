Despite countless circular-design tools coming out of academia and consultancy, many designers and engineers struggle to access and use them in action-oriented ways. This project investigated how a tool might address some of the current blind spots.

The work was heavily research-based — literature reviews and white papers alongside hands-on sessions with design engineers.
