overview: |
  I was brought in to design and co-ordinate a marketing strategy for a community
  artspace and bar, with the bottom-line aim of lifting bar revenue, especially at
  the quieter times of the week.

showOverview: true

approach: |
  I researched both the people already using the venue and the groups who never
  did. Understanding what each of them wanted from the place let me build an
  offering per need rather than one blanket promotion: board game nights for the
  midweek evenings, cake and coffee for the daytime, hot desking for the people
  working from home nearby.
