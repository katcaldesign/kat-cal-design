My first experiment with 3D modelling and animation, in 3DS Max. In Japanese culture the wings of orizuru are said to carry souls up to paradise — and, loving Japanese philosophy, I wanted to visualise the idea.

I used real 3D topology of Mount Yotei from the Japanese government, then modelled, textured and animated everything else inside 3DS Max.
